## Migration body digests exclude trailing executable SQL

**[high, confirmed]** `canonicalMigrationBody()` returns the prefix immediately before the final `INSERT INTO indicate_schema_migrations` (`src/domain/stage7/migration-manifest.ts`, lines 31–40), excluding every byte after that point. Appending `GRANT SELECT ON public.users TO indicate_runtime;` after migration 0012’s current registration leaves its checksum exactly `sha256:58dfac…c6fa7`; the original and modified content produce the same value. On a fresh database the appended statement would execute, migration 0013 would rewrite the applied row to the reviewed digest, and both local and applied-manifest readiness would pass.

Hash the whole migration except the exact self-referential checksum field, or reject anything other than whitespace/comments after a structurally parsed final registration.
